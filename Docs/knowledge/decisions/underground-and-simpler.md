# Decision

David, 2026-10-09, the first round of refining the trace:

1. **Simplify the building.** There was too much going on. The drawing keeps
   only what the event passes through: the entrance with its reader and
   camera, the controller in the riser, and the comms room with its rack.
   The occupancy and air sensors, the energy meter, the FM office and their
   cables are no longer drawn.
2. **Make the route out realistic.** The trace no longer rises into the sky.
   The cloud, the cloud AI and the third party are physical buildings, and
   the cables between them run under the ground.

# Consequences

- The section turns from a tall drawing into a street: the office on the
  left, then a data centre, an AI data centre and the third party's office.
  The data goes down the riser to the cable entry, crosses the property line
  underground, and runs in buried ducts. The run to the third party lies
  deeper and passes under the AI data centre.
- The sites are kilometres apart in reality, so the ground between them
  carries a break mark labelled "Kilometres apart". Each building is to its
  own scale; the distances are not. This replaces the old scale break into
  "the cloud".
- The event moves faster in the ducts than in the buildings, so the long
  buried stretches do not drag.
- Components that are no longer drawn stay in the knowledge base and in the
  rulebook's "alongside" column: the law still reaches them.
- The office has three storeys instead of four. The comms room stays on
  level 2, so the trace's places are unchanged.
