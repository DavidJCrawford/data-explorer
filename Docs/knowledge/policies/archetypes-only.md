# Rules

- No manufacturer, platform, hyperscaler or AI provider is named, in copy,
  data, alt text or the knowledge bundle's published parts.
- Composite-stack makers are Maker A to E and Recipient R; the governed path's
  operator is "the steward" and its host is D.
- The Trends Report article will be linked as the author's piece once it is
  published (David adds the link; until then it is not referenced on the
  site). The site does not claim that the author's employer is the steward, and the governed path is
  not drawn to resemble any real product.
- The consumer-electronics precedent may appear once as an aside, unnamed.

# Why

The argument has to stand on architecture and law. If it needs a brand, it is
marketing, and a facilities manager will read it as such.
