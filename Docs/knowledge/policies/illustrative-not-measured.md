# Rules

1. The ledger's figures come only from [the trace](/building/the-trace.md),
   where every count is defined and justified.
2. The instrument says, in its sources link and once on the final frame, that
   the system is illustrative and the counts follow from the stated
   definitions.
3. Nothing is counted that the definitions do not count. Where the counts
   undercut the thesis (both archetypes make the same number of copies), the
   site says so.
4. No percentage, risk score or "exposure index" is derived from the counts.
   A number that looks measured invites the reader to trust the wrong thing,
   which is why NFL Analysis deleted win probability.
