# Milestones

| Date | Milestone | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2024-01-11 | Entry into force | The Regulation enters into force (twentieth day after OJ publication). |
| 2025-09-12 | General application | The Act applies. Access on request (Art. 4), sharing with third parties (Art. 5), fair terms (Ch. III–IV for new obligations/contracts), switching rights (Ch. VI) all live. |
| 2026-09-12 | Data by design | Art. 3(1) design/manufacture obligations bite for connected products and related services placed on the market after this date. |
| 2027-01-12 | Switching charges end | Art. 29: providers may impose only reduced, cost-based switching charges until this date; after it, none at all. |
| 2027-09-12 | Ch. IV reaches old contracts | Unfair-terms protections extend to qualifying contracts concluded on or before 12 Sep 2025 (indefinite duration, or running ≥10 years from 11 Jan 2024). |

As of 2026-10-09 the first three have passed. Data by design (Article 3(1)) now applies to connected products and related services placed on the market after 12 September 2026.

# Pending amendment

The Commission's Digital Omnibus proposes to amend the Act. Until it is adopted, the Act applies as written. See [Digital Omnibus](/law/wider-rulebook/digital-omnibus.md).
