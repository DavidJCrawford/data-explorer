# Precise reading

Providers must take all reasonable measures (technical, organisational, legal) to prevent international governmental access/transfer of non-personal data held in the EU that would conflict with Union or national law; foreign judgments/decisions are enforceable only via international agreements or narrow conditions, with minimisation and notification duties.[^eli]
