# Precise reading

Establishes harmonised rules on: making product/related-service data available to users and third parties; data-holder obligations under Union law; unfair terms; public-sector access on exceptional need; switching between data processing services; safeguards against unlawful third-country access; and interoperability. Applies to manufacturers/providers placing connected products or related services on the EU market regardless of establishment.[^eli]

*A faithful summary of the operative demand, not the statutory text.*
