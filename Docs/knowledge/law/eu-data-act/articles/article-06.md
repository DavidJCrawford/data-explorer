# Precise reading

Third parties may process received data only for the purposes and under the conditions agreed with the user, must not use it to profile people (save as needed for the service), pass it on without agreement, or use it to develop competing connected products; they must apply agreed protections and delete when required.[^eli]

*A faithful summary of the operative demand, not the statutory text.*
