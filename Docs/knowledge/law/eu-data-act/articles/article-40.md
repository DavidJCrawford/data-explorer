# Precise reading

Member States lay down effective, proportionate, dissuasive penalties; for certain infringements (incl. Ch. II duties intersecting personal data), GDPR-style administrative fines up to EUR 20M / 4% of worldwide annual turnover apply via supervisory authorities.[^eli]
