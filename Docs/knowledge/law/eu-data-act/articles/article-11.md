# Precise reading

Data holders may apply appropriate technical protection (including smart contracts, encryption) to enforce agreed conditions — but not to hinder the user's rights; unauthorised use or onward disclosure triggers deletion/destruction duties and possible compensation.[^eli]

*A faithful summary of the operative demand, not the statutory text.*
