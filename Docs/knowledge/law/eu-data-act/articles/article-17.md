# Precise reading

Requests must be specific, transparent, proportionate, justified (need, purpose, legal basis, deadline), respect trade secrets, and be made public.[^eli]

*A faithful summary of the operative demand, not the statutory text.*

# Plain reading

The state's ask must arrive in writing, scoped and published — a well-formed request, or no request.

*Paraphrase.*
