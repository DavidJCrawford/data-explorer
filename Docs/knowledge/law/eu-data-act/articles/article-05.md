# Precise reading

On the user's request (or a party acting on their behalf), the data holder must make readily available data available to a third party the user designates — same terms as Art. 4 (without undue delay, same quality, machine-readable, continuous/real-time where applicable). Gatekeeper platforms (DMA) are not eligible third parties.[^eli]

*A faithful summary of the operative demand, not the statutory text.*

# Plain reading
