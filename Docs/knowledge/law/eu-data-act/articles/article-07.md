# Precise reading

Carves the Ch. II obligations' scope: micro/small enterprises are exempt (with anti-circumvention conditions); medium-sized enterprises get transitional accommodation; obligations cannot be contracted away to the user's detriment.[^eli]

*A faithful summary of the operative demand, not the statutory text.*

# Plain reading
