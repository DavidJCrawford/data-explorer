# Precise reading

Until 12 January 2027 providers may charge only reduced, cost-based switching charges (not exceeding costs directly linked to the switching process); from 12 January 2027, no switching charges at all.[^eli]

*A faithful summary of the operative demand, not the statutory text.*

# Plain reading
