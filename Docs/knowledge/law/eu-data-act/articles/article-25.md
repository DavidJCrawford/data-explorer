# Precise reading

Customer contracts must include switching/exit rights in writing: maximum notice, mandatory maximum transition period (30 days, extendable once if technically unfeasible), full assistance, retrieval of exportable data (minimum 30-day retrieval window post-termination), and erasure after retrieval.[^eli]

*A faithful summary of the operative demand, not the statutory text.*
