# Precise reading

Connected products and related services must be designed and provided so that product and related-service data (including metadata) are, by default, easily, securely, free of charge, and where relevant and technically feasible directly accessible to the user, in a comprehensive, structured, commonly used, machine-readable format. Pre-contract transparency duties about the data apply. Bites for products/services placed on the market after 12 Sep 2026.[^eli]

*A faithful summary of the operative demand, not the statutory text.*
