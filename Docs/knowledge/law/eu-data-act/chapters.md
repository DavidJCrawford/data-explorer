# Which chapters a facilities manager meets

- **II** — access and sharing: the rights the building's owner holds over device data.
- **III–IV** — the terms: fair, reasonable and non-discriminatory sharing, and unfair clauses struck down.
- **VI–VII** — the cloud: switching, exit charges, published jurisdictions, protection from foreign access.
- **X** — the database right cannot be used to lock the event log away.

Chapters V (public-sector access), VIII (interoperability), IX (enforcement) and XI (final provisions) are context. The experience shows them in the reference, not on the trace.
