# What applies

- **Privacy Act 1988**, as amended by the Privacy and Other Legislation
  Amendment Act 2024. From 10 December 2026, privacy policies must disclose
  where computer programs make, or substantially support, decisions that could
  significantly affect people's rights or interests using their personal
  information.[^lander] For a building, that can reach automated access
  decisions and AI on occupancy.
- **Further reform** is in consultation; status unverified.[^mk]
- **Consumer Data Right.** Sector-based (banking and energy retail). It does
  not cover building device data.
- **EU reach.** As for the other jurisdictions.

Unverified, to research before use: whether critical-infrastructure security
law reaches commercial buildings' systems.
