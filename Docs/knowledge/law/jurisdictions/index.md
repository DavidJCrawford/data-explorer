# Jurisdictions

The rulebook can be switched between jurisdictions. Only the EU has a general right to connected-device data; elsewhere the rows are empty or sector-limited, and the page says so in words. All entries outside the EU are first drafts.

* [European Union](european-union.md) - The general right. The default view.
* [United Kingdom](united-kingdom.md) - Smart-data powers, no building scheme.
* [New Zealand](new-zealand.md) - Sector designations: banking now, electricity planned.
* [Australia](australia.md) - Privacy reform with automated-decision transparency from December 2026.
* [United States](united-states.md) - No device-data right; state privacy laws for personal information.
