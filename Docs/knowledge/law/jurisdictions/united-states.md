# What applies

- **No general right** to connected-product data at federal level.
- **State privacy laws** such as California's CCPA give consumers rights to
  access, delete and limit the use of their personal information.[^ccpa] They
  reach some building data, such as access events about identifiable people,
  but they are rights of the person the data is about, not of the building's
  owner.
- **EU reach.** A US manufacturer selling into the EU is bound by the EU Data
  Act for those products.
