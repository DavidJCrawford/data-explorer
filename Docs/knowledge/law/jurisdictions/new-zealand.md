# What applies

- **Customer and Product Data Act 2025.** In force from 30 March 2025.[^cpd_act]
  Rights apply only to sectors the government designates. Banking was
  designated from 1 December 2025.[^banking] Electricity retail is planned to
  follow, with data holders providing data from 1 July 2027.[^electricity]
- **Personal data.** The Privacy Act 2020.
- **EU reach.** A New Zealand manufacturer selling connected products into the
  EU is bound by the EU Data Act for those products.

# On the rulebook
