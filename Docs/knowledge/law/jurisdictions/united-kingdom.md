# What applies

- **Smart data powers.** The Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 received Royal
  Assent on 19 June 2025. Part 1, which lets the government create schemes
  giving customers access to their data in named sectors, commenced on 20
  August 2025.[^scl] No scheme covering building systems or connected devices
  was found as of October 2026.[^kennedys]
- **Personal data.** UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, amended in stages
  by the 2025 Act through 2026.[^duaa_plan]
- **EU reach.** A UK manufacturer selling into the EU is bound by the EU Data
  Act for those products.

# On the rulebook

The Data Act rows show as *no equivalent general right*, with the smart-data
powers noted. The honest absence is the point.

Unverified, to research before use: the UK's network-security regime for
critical sectors and its product-security regime for consumer connectable
products, and whether either reaches commercial building systems.
