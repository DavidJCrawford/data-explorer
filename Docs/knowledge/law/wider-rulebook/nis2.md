# The question it answers

**Whose duty is it to ask?**

The EU's cybersecurity directive for essential and important entities — national law since October 2024.

# For a facilities manager

If your organisation is in scope — energy, health, transport, water, public administration and more — the security of your suppliers is your own legal duty, not diligence: Article 21(2)(d) makes you responsible for supply-chain security, supplier by supplier, including the makers of your building's systems. Every question on this page then becomes your compliance evidence.

# The ask

> Ask yourself first: are we an essential or important entity? Then ask the vendor for the supplier-security evidence your own regulator will one day ask you for.
