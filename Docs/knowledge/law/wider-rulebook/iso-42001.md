# The question it answers

**Is the organisation behind the AI disciplined?**

The certifiable AI management-system standard — it certifies the organisation, never the product.

# For a facilities manager

A certificate tells you the vendor develops and runs its AI under audited discipline: lifecycle management, risk and impact assessment, oversight of its own AI suppliers. It cannot tell you a product is safe — no certificate can — but it tells you someone is answerable, on a schedule, for the question. The scope statement decides whether it covers the AI you're actually buying.

# The ask

> Is your AI management system certified to ISO/IEC 42001 — and does the certificate's scope cover this product's AI features?
