# The question it answers

**Is the hardware born secure — and who patches it?**

The EU law making security-by-design and vulnerability handling legal duties for products with digital elements.

# For a facilities manager

The Data Act makes your devices born open (September 2026); the CRA makes them born secure. Manufacturers must report actively exploited vulnerabilities from 11 September 2026, and meet the full duties — secure by design, a declared support period, security updates for it — from 11 December 2027, with CE marking to show it.

# The ask

> What is the declared support period for each device — and how do security updates reach a controller that never touches the internet?
