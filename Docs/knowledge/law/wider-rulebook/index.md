# The wider rulebook

The instruments around the Data Act, each reduced to the one question it answers and the one thing it lets a facilities manager ask. None grants the buyer rights over device data; that is the Data Act's job.

* [GDPR](gdpr.md) - Is this data about people?
* [NIS2](nis2.md) - Whose duty is it to ask?
* [EU AI Act](ai-act.md) - What may the AI legally do to the people here?
* [ISO/IEC 42001](iso-42001.md) - Is the organisation behind the AI disciplined?
* [Cyber Resilience Act](cra.md) - Is the hardware born secure, and who patches it?
* [SOC 2 · ISO/IEC 27001](soc2-27001.md) - Is the cloud disciplined with my data?
