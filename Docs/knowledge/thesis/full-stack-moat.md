# Summary

Background for the long-form article, not content for the site. Three moats
compound for a vendor that makes the hardware and runs the software:

1. **Physical presence.** Controllers, readers, certifications and installer
   networks with installs that last decades. AI erodes this slowest.
2. **Data and domain insight.** Seeing the whole picture from the device to the
   application.
3. **Trust and governance.** Compliance, audit and reliability as the product.

The [Data Act](/law/eu-data-act/overview.md) deliberately weakens the second as
*possession*: the customer can take the data to a competitor. It survives only
as *stewardship*, being the party the customer keeps choosing. That reframe is
the bridge between this strategy and the [article](/thesis/the-article.md).
