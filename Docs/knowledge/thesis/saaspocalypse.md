# Summary

Background for the long-form article only. In January 2026 agentic-AI launches
prompted a broad repricing of software stocks on the question of whether agents
could do the workflows that per-seat software sells. What was repriced hardest
was thin, workflow-only software. What held was infrastructure: compliance,
security, audit, reliability and anything tied to the physical world.

The figures in the prototype's note (market value lost, multiples) are unverified
here and must be re-sourced before any appear in the long-form article.
