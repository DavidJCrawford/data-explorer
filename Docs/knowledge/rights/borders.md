# The right

**Your data can't be quietly reached across borders.** Providers must publish where it lives and shield it from unlawful foreign access.

The jurisdictions your data sits in must be published, and your provider must take real measures against foreign governmental access that would conflict with EU or national law.[^eli]

[Article 28](/law/eu-data-act/articles/article-28.md), [Article 32](/law/eu-data-act/articles/article-32.md). Applies from 2025-09-12.
