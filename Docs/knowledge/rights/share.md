# The right

**You can send it to anyone — even their competitor.** Point at a third party; the vendor must deliver your data to them on the same terms.

A rival analytics platform, an energy optimiser, your own consultant: you designate, they receive. The receiving party carries duties too — purpose-bound use, no cloning the product it came from.[^eli]
