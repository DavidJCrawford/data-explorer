# The seven rights

What the EU Data Act gives a connected building's owner or operator, in the second person, each backed by articles and placed in the building.

* [The data your building makes is yours to take.](yours.md) - Free, machine-readable, continuous — on request, at the same quality the vendor sees.
* [You can send it to anyone — even their competitor.](share.md) - Point at a third party; the vendor must deliver your data to them on the same terms.
* [New devices must be born open.](born-open.md) - Products placed on the market after Sep 2026 must make their data accessible by design.
* [Leaving is your right — and the exit fee is dying.](leave.md) - Switching between cloud services is protected now; switching charges end 12 Jan 2027.
* [Take-it-or-leave-it data clauses can't bind you.](fair-terms.md) - Unfair terms imposed unilaterally about your data are void — old contracts get caught too.
* [Your data can't be quietly reached across borders.](borders.md) - Providers must publish where it lives and shield it from unlawful foreign access.
* [The event log isn’t their database — it’s your data.](event-log.md) - Vendors can't rebuild lock-in by claiming database rights over what your building generated.
