# The right

**Leaving is your right — and the exit fee is dying.** Switching between cloud services is protected now; switching charges end 12 Jan 2027.

Contracts must spell out the exit: notice, a maximum transition period, retrieval of your data, then provable erasure. Until January 2027 they may charge only their actual costs; after that, nothing.[^eli]
