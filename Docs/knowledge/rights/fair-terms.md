# The right

**Take-it-or-leave-it data clauses can't bind you.** Unfair terms imposed unilaterally about your data are void — old contracts get caught too.

The fine print stops being a moat. Unfair unilateral clauses on data access and use don't bind, and from September 2027 the protection reaches qualifying contracts signed before the Act applied.[^eli]
