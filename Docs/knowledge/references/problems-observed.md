# The inventory

From industry press, practitioner forums and vendor comparisons, July 2026.
Background for the long-form article; individual figures are unverified here.

1. **Deployment options narrow fast.** Many cloud access-control products are
   cloud-only; organisations that need on-premises or hybrid find the shortlist
   collapses.
2. **Nobody owns surveillance policy.** Who may review or export video sits
   between security, IT and legal.
3. **Training anxiety.** "Is our data training your models?" is a standard
   questionnaire item with few crisp answers.
4. **Regulations stack.** Obligations attach at every hop, and nothing shows
   the whole path.
5. **Lock-in resentment.** Hardware lock-in and siloed software are the most
   frequent complaints.
6. **Egress and hidden costs.** Exporting data, or leaving, is priced.
7. **Retention as a sales artefact.** Retention is set by the tier, not by the
   customer.
8. **Fragmentation.** Several vendors, several alert streams, no shared model.
9. **Custody cannot be shown.** Everyone says the data is safe; almost nobody
   can show the chain.

# Which the site answers

4 and 9 directly: the trace shows the whole path and the chain. 5 and 6 are
what the Data Act's switching chapter ends. 3 is the last hop.
