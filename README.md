# Data Explorer

Where a connected building's data goes, from the door to the cloud, and what the
law says the building's owner is owed at each step of the way.

**Status:** specification and knowledge base. No site yet.

- [Docs/SPEC.md](Docs/SPEC.md) is the specification and the build tracker.
- [Docs/knowledge/](Docs/knowledge/index.md) is the knowledge base, an
  [Open Knowledge Format](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/open-knowledge-format)
  v0.2 bundle. It holds all of the site's content. Pages are compiled from it.

Successor to [stewardship-explorer](https://github.com/DavidJCrawford/stewardship-explorer),
whose content it inherits. It follows the design system of
[The Longest Journey](https://github.com/DavidJCrawford/the-longest-journey),
[F1 Analysis](https://github.com/DavidJCrawford/f1-analysis) and
[NFL Analysis](https://github.com/DavidJCrawford/nfl-analysis).

Not legal advice.
